For Schools

Support your students' wellbeing with an application designed for New Zealand schools.

How to introduce it

  1. Get in touch — Tell us about your school and we'll explain how it works.
  2. Plan the rollout — Work with our team on the best way to introduce it to students.
  3. Support your staff — We help teachers and counsellors use the application alongside students.
  4. Check in & review — See how it's being used and adapt as needed.
