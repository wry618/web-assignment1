About StressExterminators

Who we are, how we work, and what we stand for.

The team

StressExterminators was developed by a team of New Zealand school psychologists. We bring experience working with young people in school settings and an understanding of the pressures they face.

Team member details and qualifications are not listed here — they should be provided and verified by the team.

The approach

Our method is a cognitive behaviour therapy (CBT) approach developed specifically for the New Zealand school context. It focuses on practical, skill-based activities that young people can use to understand and manage stress.
