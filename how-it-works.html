How it works

A clear, three-step path to build skills for managing everyday stress.

1

Understand

Learn what stress is, what it feels like for you, and what triggers it — in short, easy lessons.

2

Learn skills

Practise CBT-based skills designed for young people in New Zealand, like noticing thoughts and shifting them.

3

Practise & track

Use short daily activities and track how you feel over time to see what helps.

Explore each step

Understand your stress

Short lessons explain what stress is and how it shows up in your body and thoughts. You'll learn to spot your own early signs.

Learn CBT skills

Guided activities introduce cognitive behaviour therapy skills — like noticing unhelpful thoughts and practising kinder, more balanced ways of thinking.

Practise & track

Quick check-ins and short exercises help you practise regularly and see your progress over time.

Ready to try it?

Start with step one and build your skills at your own pace.
